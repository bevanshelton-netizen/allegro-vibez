# ALLEGRO-VIBEZ — RIGHTS-SAFE MODE

## Purpose
ALLEGRO-VIBEZ Phase 1 is a promotion, discovery, artist-development, chart, radio, festival and opportunity platform. It is **not** a collecting society, royalty administrator, publishing administrator, mechanical-rights administrator, or rights marketplace.

## Core rule
Artists retain ownership and control of their music unless a separate written agreement expressly states otherwise.

ALLEGRO-VIBEZ does not acquire copyright, master ownership, publishing rights, performance rights or royalty-collection rights merely because an artist uploads or promotes content on the platform.

## Rights declaration
Before submitting music for promotional use, the submitting party must confirm one of the following:

1. **OWNED / CONTROLLED** — the submitting party owns or controls the relevant rights; or
2. **AUTHORISED** — the submitting party has permission from the relevant rights holder(s) for the specific promotional use.

The platform should retain the declaration, submitter identity, date, scope of permission and supporting evidence metadata where applicable.

## Promotion-only designation
Content may be designated **PROMOTION ONLY**. This means ALLEGRO-VIBEZ may use the content only for the expressly authorised promotional purpose and does not receive a general licence or ownership interest.

## Radio safety
Radio and broadcast features must use music that is appropriately licensed, cleared, supplied under permission, or otherwise lawful for the intended programming model. ALLEGRO-VIBEZ must not imply that operating a promotional radio/discovery service automatically resolves third-party performance or communication-to-public rights.

## Festival safety
Festival and booking submissions remain subject to the artist's and/or relevant rights holder's obligations for performance permissions, clearances and contractual requirements. ALLEGRO-VIBEZ does not represent that a festival submission itself clears those obligations.

## Charts
ALLEGRO-VIBEZ charts are proprietary platform charts unless and until a separate industry authority or official chart relationship is established. Rankings must use a published methodology and verified signals. Paid exposure may buy a marketing service but must not directly purchase a chart position.

Chart inputs may include eligible platform activity, verified fan engagement, approved radio exposure, saves/follows, campaign performance, bookings and territory growth. Artificial activity, manipulated votes and fraudulent streams must be excluded.

## Awards
ALLEGRO-VIBEZ awards recognise performance within the ALLEGRO-VIBEZ chart and opportunity ecosystem. Awards must not be presented as official industry certifications unless an authorised external body independently confirms that status.

### Explicit exclusions at launch
Do not market ALLEGRO-VIBEZ as:
- a royalty collection agency;
- a collecting society / PRO;
- a publishing administrator;
- a mechanical-rights administrator;
- a royalty escrow service;
- a rights marketplace;
- a music distributor assuming rights clearance; or
- a service guaranteeing royalty income from streams or airplay.

## Phase 2 / Phase 3 gate
Rights administration, distribution and royalty infrastructure may only be introduced as separate products after deliberate legal, commercial and operational review, with appropriate contracts, licensing, reporting and external rights infrastructure.

## Product language rule
Prefer:
- "artist earnings" when referring to documented platform/service revenue payable under an agreement;
- "promotion" and "exposure" for marketing services;
- "chart points" and "verified activity" for chart methodology;
- "rights declaration" and "permission status" for submitted content.

Avoid unsupported claims such as "we collect your royalties," "we own your masters," "we clear all rights," or "airplay automatically generates royalties through ALLEGRO-VIBEZ."

## Principle
**Build the audience, network and artist business first. Do not inherit rights complexity before the platform needs it.**
