If payment is cancelled or fails before successful verification, paid access is not activated. A payment should not be treated as complete until iKhokha and the Academy confirmation flow show a successful status.

If the same intended Academy pass is charged twice, or an incorrect amount is charged because of a platform error, contact us promptly with the transaction reference. Once verified, the duplicate or incorrect charge will be corrected or refunded as appropriate.

Digital access after activation

The Academy pass is digital access made available after successful payment verification. Refund requests after activation will be considered where the paid service was materially unavailable, materially misdescribed, duplicated, or where applicable law requires a remedy.

The launch pass covers 30 days and does not automatically renew. There is therefore no recurring Academy debit to cancel under this launch offer. Rights that cannot lawfully be excluded remain unaffected.

If paid Academy access cannot be delivered because of a platform fault that cannot be restored within a reasonable period, we may extend access, provide an equivalent remedy, or refund the affected payment depending on the circumstances.

Email info@izakhonoafrica.co.za with the purchaser name, payment date and iKhokha transaction reference. Do not send card numbers, PINs, passwords or CVV information.
