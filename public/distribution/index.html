ALLEGRO-VIBEZ is preparing a controlled distribution layer for creator releases. Delivery packages, metadata manifests and external acknowledgements remain separate from internal readiness so creators always know what has actually happened.

01 · PREPARE

Release Manifest

Package release metadata, tracks, artwork, identifiers, credits and rights information into a distributor-ready specification.

02 · DELIVER

Distribution Gateway

Designed for future integrations with approved distribution partners. No third-party delivery is represented until an external acknowledgement is verified.

03 · VERIFY

Delivery Status

Track submission, acknowledgement, validation, rejection, correction and delivery states with an auditable history.

Verification ruleALLEGRO-VIBEZ will never show a release as delivered to a DSP merely because a creator clicked submit. External delivery requires a verifiable partner response.
