Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective 17 September 2026

Merchant and payment provider

ALLEGRO-VIBEZ commercial packages are sold by IZAKHONO AFRICA (PTY) LTD. Eligible online payments are processed through iKhokha. ALLEGRO-VIBEZ does not collect card numbers, PINs, CVVs, banking passwords or OTPs.

Creator and launch packages

If a paid service has not yet started, you may request cancellation and a refund. Once work has begun, any refund will take account of work already completed and non-recoverable third-party costs, where applicable. If ALLEGRO-VIBEZ cannot deliver a paid service as agreed, an appropriate refund, credit or replacement service will be offered.

Radio packages

Radio inventory may be reserved before payment. Radio packages do not become payable until ALLEGRO confirms verified station activation and the booked campaign. Once a paid campaign begins, missed booked plays will first be remedied through make-good inventory where reasonably possible. If contracted inventory cannot be delivered, the undelivered portion may be refunded or credited.

How to request a refund

Use the contact details or booking channel supplied with your ALLEGRO-VIBEZ order and provide your name, package, payment reference and reason for the request. Approved refunds are returned through the appropriate payment route and may be subject to normal payment-provider processing times.

Consumer rights

This policy does not limit any rights that apply under South African consumer law.
