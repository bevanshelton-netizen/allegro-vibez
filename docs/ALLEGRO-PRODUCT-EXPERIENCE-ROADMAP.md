# ALLEGRO-VIBEZ — Product Experience Roadmap

## Product standard
ALLEGRO-VIBEZ is being developed as a professional music-business operating system, not merely a music upload site. Every authenticated screen must feel like one coherent premium product.

## Experience architecture
1. **Public Discovery** — artist discovery, catalogue discovery, opportunities and culture.
2. **Artist OS** — private command centre for career, catalogue, money, rights, bookings and team.
3. **Creator Studio** — release creation, artwork, audio, metadata, contributors and submission workflow.
4. **Release Management** — readiness, moderation, rights, delivery status and release history.
5. **Rights Centre** — ownership, contributors, publishing/master splits and evidence.
6. **Wallet** — earnings, pending balances, transactions, royalty records and payout readiness.
7. **Bookings** — opportunities, booking pipeline, contracts, deposits, settlement and history.
8. **Management** — local and international career representation pathways.

## Design language
- Dark luxury foundation with restrained African-inspired green and gold accents.
- Strong editorial typography and generous spacing.
- Cards should communicate hierarchy rather than create visual clutter.
- Status, money and workflow states must be immediately legible.
- Mobile experience is first-class, not a compressed desktop layout.
- Existing business logic and routes must remain intact while presentation is upgraded.

## Build sequence
### Phase 1 — Command centre
Artist OS navigation, overview, profile, catalogue, opportunities, collaboration, bookings, money and career controls.

### Phase 2 — Creator Studio
Premium upload workspace with release checklist, audio/artwork metadata, contributor and rights capture, save-draft and submit-for-review states.

### Phase 3 — Release & rights
A single release record becomes the source of truth across catalogue, rights, moderation and discovery.

### Phase 4 — Financial centre
Wallet ledger, royalty records, booking income, pending/available/paid states and payout readiness.

### Phase 5 — Commercial network
Bookings, management, collaboration and discovery connect artists to real commercial opportunities.

## Non-negotiables
- Do not replace working flows merely for visual redesign.
- Do not fabricate earnings, plays, bookings or rights data.
- Demo/empty states must be clearly identified.
- Production status is never claimed without sovereign runtime evidence.
- IZAKHONO-owned infrastructure remains the preferred production path.
