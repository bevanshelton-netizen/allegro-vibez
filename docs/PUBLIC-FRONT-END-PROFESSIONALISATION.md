# ALLEGRO VIBEZ — Public Front-End Professionalisation

## Objective

Position ALLEGRO VIBEZ as a premium African/global music business platform at first glance. The public experience must communicate music, opportunity, creator prosperity, trust and cultural confidence before users encounter internal command-centre tooling.

## Public experience priorities

1. **Hero** — cinematic, premium and immediately understandable. Lead with ALLEGRO VIBEZ and a clear proposition for artists, music lovers and industry partners.
2. **Primary actions** — Discover Music, Join as an Artist, Explore Opportunities and Listen/Radio must be visually prominent and mobile-friendly.
3. **Artist prosperity** — Present ALLEGRO as more than a streaming catalogue: careers, rights, releases, revenue, bookings, education and global opportunities.
4. **African identity, global standard** — retain the African heritage identity without making the interface feel regional or dated.
5. **Trust** — rights-safe music, transparent creator economics, professional profiles and secure account journeys should be visible public signals.
6. **Discovery** — surface artists, releases, radio, festivals and opportunities with strong editorial hierarchy rather than dashboard-heavy presentation.
7. **Conversion** — every major public section should lead naturally to one next action: discover, listen, join, release, book, learn or partner.
8. **Mobile first** — cards, navigation, typography, hero media and calls-to-action must remain polished on small screens.
9. **Performance** — visual richness must not become a reason for slow page loading. Prefer lightweight assets, lazy loading and progressive enhancement.
10. **SEO/social** — maintain authoritative titles, descriptions, canonical metadata and share previews for the public domain.

## Brand direction

The existing cinematic/Hollywood marquee and vibrant visual work are retained as reference material, but the public experience should mature into a coherent premium system rather than accumulating disconnected visual treatments.

The target impression is: **African music. World-class business. Artist prosperity.**

## Information architecture

Public navigation should prioritise:

- Home
- Discover
- Radio
- Artists
- Releases
- Opportunities
- Festivals
- Education
- About

Primary conversion: **Join ALLEGRO**.

Artist-facing authenticated tools remain behind authentication and should not dominate the public landing experience.

## Acceptance standard

A public-facing refinement is complete only when the resulting interface is visually coherent, responsive, understandable without explanation, commercially credible and consistent with the ALLEGRO brand. No claim of public launch is made by this document; deployment remains subject to the IZAKHONO owned-runtime production acceptance gates.
