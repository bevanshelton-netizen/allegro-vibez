# Legacy Artist Activation Engine

The activation layer turns an approved engagement into a controlled 90-day relaunch.

## Gate
Activation is permitted only after an approved or approved-with-conditions decision. The engine does not assume ownership, clearance, revenue, partnerships or bookings.

## 90-day operating model
1. Reconnect — career story, catalogue inventory, brand assets and fan reconnection.
2. Relaunch — content engine, radio/media, chart preparation, KORA exposure and press.
3. Commercialise — bookings, festivals, sponsors, licensing opportunities and target markets.

## Evidence rule
Every completed action should have evidence where appropriate. Results must be verified before being presented as completed commercial outcomes.

## Rights rule
The artist/rightsholder retains rights unless a separate, explicit agreement says otherwise. Catalogue, master, publishing and licensing activity remains subject to documented rights verification.

## Frontend
`app/legacy-activation.html` is the authenticated activation workspace. It is intended to sit behind the existing ALLEGRO-VIBEZ command-centre authentication flow.
