# ALLEGRO ↔ KORA CREATOR FLOW

## One creator journey

1. Artist joins ALLEGRO.
2. Global Artist Passport captures country, region, languages, genres and booking availability.
3. Artist chooses career path: LAUNCH / GROW / REVIVE / RELAUNCH.
4. ARTIST PROTECT / ALLEGRO rights workflow records contributors and release clearance.
5. Cleared audio enters ALLEGRO discovery/radio consideration.
6. Artist marketing space drives discovery, press and bookings.
7. If screen content exists, creator links their KORA creator account.
8. ALLEGRO sends the video metadata through a server-side handoff.
9. KORA creates the screen asset as draft + rights review + moderation pending.
10. KORA monetisation can use free, ad-supported, premium or PPV access when commercial/payment gates are active.
11. Touring artists use Tour2Screen to convert tour stops into episodes, streams, films and sponsor assets.
12. Revenue returns through the configured platform ledger and creator share rules.

## Hard boundaries

- ALLEGRO never exposes KORA integration/video-provider secrets in the browser.
- KORA never auto-publishes a handoff.
- A public video is not automatically rights-cleared.
- A career-path selection is strategy metadata, not a promise of success or airplay.
- Payment/PPV remains gated by the real production payment state.
