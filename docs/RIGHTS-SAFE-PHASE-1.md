# ALLEGRO-VIBEZ — Rights-Safe Phase 1

## Purpose
ALLEGRO-VIBEZ Phase 1 is a promotion, discovery, chart, artist-development and opportunity platform. It deliberately avoids operating as a royalty-collection or rights-administration business.

## Artist rights
- Artists retain ownership/control of their music and other submitted works unless a separate written agreement expressly states otherwise.
- Submission to ALLEGRO-VIBEZ does not transfer copyright, publishing, master ownership or neighbouring rights.
- ALLEGRO-VIBEZ may request evidence that an artist owns or is authorised to submit material.

## Rights declaration
Every release submission should record:
- submitting party
- artist
- title/version
- master-control declaration
- publishing/control declaration where applicable
- third-party material declaration
- permission/clearance status
- promotional-use permission
- date of declaration
- evidence/reference notes where supplied

## Promotional use
Material may be marked **PROMOTION-ONLY**. This permits the platform to use approved material for discovery, editorial promotion, chart presentation, radio servicing, festival submissions and marketing only to the extent authorised by the applicable submission terms.

## Explicitly out of scope for Phase 1
ALLEGRO-VIBEZ will not initially:
- collect royalties on behalf of artists;
- operate as a collecting society/PRO;
- administer publishing rights;
- administer mechanical rights;
- operate a royalty escrow service;
- acquire master rights by default;
- promise streaming royalty income;
- represent that third-party DSP distribution is live without a verified provider integration;
- license music to third parties without an appropriate agreement.

## Radio safety
Radio activity should use artist submissions, appropriately licensed/permissioned programming and approved promotional material. Station relationships must record the applicable permission/licence status. A station prospect is not a partner until the relationship is confirmed.

## Festival safety
ALLEGRO-VIBEZ may identify and facilitate opportunities, but the artist remains responsible for performance-rights, repertoire and other applicable clearances unless a separate written agreement assigns responsibility elsewhere.

## Charts
Charts measure verified ALLEGRO-VIBEZ activity and other documented signals under a published methodology. They are not represented as official national or industry-wide charts unless a separate recognised partnership establishes that status.

## Anti-manipulation
No artist may purchase a chart position. Artificial streams, bots, manipulated votes, fraudulent engagement and other integrity violations must be excluded and may trigger review or removal.

## Commercial model
Artist-first commercial plans may charge for services such as development, promotion, EPK creation, radio servicing, campaign management, analytics and management. Any revenue share must be clearly documented and must not be represented as royalty collection.

## Phase 2 gate
Any future distribution, licensing or rights-management capability requires a separate legal/commercial design, provider agreements, rights metadata, reporting controls and verified integration before being described as live.
