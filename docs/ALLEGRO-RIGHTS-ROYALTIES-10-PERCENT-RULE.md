# ALLEGRO RIGHTS & ROYALTIES

## The 10% Rule

ALLEGRO retains a **10% administration/platform fee** on royalty money actually collected through the ALLEGRO rights and royalties service.

**Creator receives: 90% of the collected royalty amount.**

This rule is commercial policy. It does **not** transfer copyright ownership to ALLEGRO.

### Example

If ALLEGRO collects R10,000 for a creator:

- Gross collected: R10,000
- ALLEGRO fee (10%): R1,000
- Creator royalty pool (90%): R9,000

If the creator has contributors, the R9,000 creator pool is allocated according to the recorded ownership/split data.

## Product

The ALLEGRO Rights & Royalties engine is designed to cover:

1. **Catalogue Vault** — compositions, recordings, identifiers, writers, producers, publishers and ownership/splits.
2. **Registration Control** — registration status by society, platform and territory.
3. **Royalty Radar** — incoming statements and usage data mapped to repertoire.
4. **Missing Money Detector** — unmatched, unregistered or suspicious royalty lines for investigation.
5. **Split Engine** — applies verified ownership percentages to the 90% creator pool.
6. **Artist Wallet** — gross, ALLEGRO fee, creator net, pending, collected and paid.
7. **Statement Ledger** — immutable references to source, territory, currency and statement period.
8. **Audit Trail** — every adjustment and payout should be traceable.

## Legal / commercial boundary

ALLEGRO's software can manage metadata, registration workflows, matching, statements and royalty accounting. Actual worldwide collection requires appropriate contracts, society/CMO relationships, DSP/licensing arrangements and any required publishing-administration permissions.

The platform must therefore distinguish between:

- **Technology:** ALLEGRO Rights & Royalties software and Money Map.
- **Collection authority:** the contracted entity or licensed collection partner actually entitled to receive and administer the money.

Never represent software capability as a legal collection mandate.
