# ALLEGRO-VIBEZ

## More Than Music. A Movement.

**African-born. Global by design.**

ALLEGRO-VIBEZ is being rebuilt from the original product vision as a global artist prosperity platform — not as a marketing website, not as a collection of disconnected tools, and not as a deployment experiment.

### Core mission
Give African and global creators a serious home to **create, release, discover, connect, grow, and earn**.

### Product pillars
- Music discovery and listening
- Artist and creator profiles
- Music creation and release management
- Fan/community relationships
- Playlists and discovery
- Artist career development
- Rights, ownership and royalty infrastructure
- Creator earnings and payments
- Opportunities, collaboration and bookings
- Culture, media and commerce
- Global distribution readiness

### Build principles
1. Product first.
2. Creator prosperity first.
3. African-born, global-ready.
4. Ownership and portability over vendor lock-in.
5. Real integrations only; never simulate live functionality.
6. Security and privacy by design.
7. Mobile-first and low-bandwidth conscious.
8. Build the simplest correct foundation before adding complexity.

## Rebuild rule
The previous implementation is **not the product specification**. This branch is a clean rebuild from the original ALLEGRO-VIBEZ vision. Previous deployment, merchandising, distribution and application architecture are intentionally excluded from this foundation.
