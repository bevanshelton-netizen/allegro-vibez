# ALLEGRO ADVERTISER OUTREACH

## Positioning

Do not sell "cheap radio". Sell:
- founding access to a new African creator/audience platform;
- measurable proof-of-play;
- low-risk trial pricing;
- web + audio visibility;
- category exclusivity where available;
- make-good protection;
- creative assistance;
- no radio-airtime payment before verified activation.

## Best first prospects

Prioritise:
1. retailers and FMCG;
2. financial services and fintech;
3. mobile/data and consumer technology;
4. transport/automotive;
5. restaurants/food;
6. fashion/beauty;
7. events/entertainment;
8. education/training;
9. local services near launch geography;
10. brands already connected to IZAKHONO relationships.

## Qualification questions

- What are you promoting in the next 30–90 days?
- Which customer segment matters most?
- National, Gauteng or local campaign?
- Is the objective awareness, foot traffic, leads, event attendance or sales?
- Do you already have a 30-second audio creative?
- Which category exclusivity would be valuable?
- What result would make a trial successful?

## Follow-up

Day 0: concise founding offer.
Day 3: one useful campaign suggestion, no generic "checking in".
Day 7: final founding-inventory note.
Stop after that unless they engage.
