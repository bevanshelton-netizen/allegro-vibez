# ALLEGRO 10% COMMERCIAL RULE

## Locked default

For creator/artist revenue **processed or administered through ALLEGRO**, the default platform share is:

**ALLEGRO: 10%**  
**Creator/rightsholder side: 90%**

The database stores this as **1,000 basis points**.

## What the 10% applies to

Examples:
- creator royalties recorded through ALLEGRO;
- creator monetisation proceeds administered by ALLEGRO;
- eligible artist booking/marketplace proceeds where ALLEGRO is acting as the platform/intermediary;
- other creator revenue streams that are expressly brought into the ALLEGRO commercial ledger.

## What is not a 10/90 split

Revenue that ALLEGRO earns in its own right is not treated as artist money. Examples:
- ALLEGRO radio advertising;
- show sponsorships;
- ALLEGRO platform subscriptions;
- ALLEGRO training/academy fees;
- ALLEGRO-owned promotional/service packages.

Those amounts are ALLEGRO revenue, subject to normal delivery costs, provider fees and tax obligations.

## Overrides

The 10% creator platform share may only change when:
1. there is a specific commercial reason;
2. the artist/partner agrees;
3. the agreement is in writing;
4. the system stores an agreement reference;
5. an authorised administrator activates the override.

No silent plan tier may reduce the platform fee.

## Refunds and reversals

Fee calculations should follow the actual recognised creator gross revenue after legitimate reversals/refunds are recorded. Tax, statutory collections and money that never beneficially belongs to the creator should not be misclassified as creator gross revenue.

## Artist protection

The artist dashboard must show:
- gross creator revenue;
- ALLEGRO 10% platform share;
- creator net before any separate tax/reserve choices;
- transaction source and statement period;
- any signed fee override.

The artist should never discover the fee only after requesting payout.
