# ALLEGRO RADIO — ADVERTISER AGREEMENT HEADS OF TERMS

The final agreement should cover:

1. Advertiser legal name, registration/VAT details and authorised contact.
2. Campaign, dates, target inventory and agreed number of spots.
3. Creative duration and technical specifications.
4. Price, taxes, payment terms and late-payment consequences.
5. Proof-of-play report as ALLEGRO's delivery evidence.
6. Make-good mechanism for confirmed under-delivery.
7. Advertiser warranty that all claims, offers, prices, endorsements and required disclosures are lawful, accurate and substantiated.
8. Sector-specific approval requirements for regulated products/services.
9. No unlawful discrimination, hate speech, deception, unsafe claims or undisclosed paid editorial.
10. ALLEGRO right to refuse, pause or remove creative that creates legal, regulatory, brand-safety or rights risk.
11. IP licence allowing ALLEGRO to transmit the approved ad during the campaign.
12. Data/privacy rules: advertiser receives campaign statistics, not listener personal data unless a separate lawful data-sharing arrangement exists.
13. Cancellation, suspension, force majeure and technical-failure rules.
14. Complaints, governing law and dispute resolution.
