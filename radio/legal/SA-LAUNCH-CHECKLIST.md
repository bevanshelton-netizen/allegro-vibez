# ALLEGRO RADIO — SOUTH AFRICA LEGAL LAUNCH CHECKLIST

This is an operational checklist, not a substitute for advice from a South African media/copyright lawyer.

## 1. Internet-only architecture first

Launch ALLEGRO Radio as an **internet-only webcasting service**. Do not transmit on FM/AM/DAB or use radio-frequency spectrum without the required ICASA authority.

ICASA publicly distinguished internet/web radio from terrestrial digital sound broadcasting and stated that online/Internet radio was not then regulated as broadcasting. Because that statement predates this launch, obtain written confirmation of the current 2026 position for ALLEGRO's exact internet-only architecture before describing the service as exempt or unlicensed.

Keep:
- written ICASA query and response
- architecture diagram showing internet-only delivery
- no terrestrial transmitter/spectrum use
- hosting and stream endpoint details

## 2. Music composition rights — SAMRO

Apply to SAMRO as a music user for the licence applicable to internet radio / mobile and internet transmission and/or broadcasting, based on SAMRO's current classification.

SAMRO administers composers', authors' and publishers' performing rights.

Keep:
- licence agreement
- invoices and proof of payment
- monthly/periodic playlist logs
- title, composer/publisher metadata where available
- duration and timestamps of each musical work played

Contact/current application route: https://musicuser.samro.org.za/

## 3. Sound recording / performer rights — SAMPRA

Obtain the SAMPRA licence applicable to online radio before broadcasting commercial sound recordings in SAMPRA's repertoire.

For 2026, SAMPRA publishes Tariff 35-01 for online radio stations. The published tariff states a fixed annual fee plus a percentage of net revenue from advertising, sponsorships and subscriptions. Verify the current invoice and tariff directly with SAMPRA before payment or launch.

Keep:
- SAMPRA licence
- tariff version
- invoices and proof of payment
- track-level playout logs
- revenue records used to calculate any variable royalty component

Application/contact: https://sampra.org.za/music-users/

## 4. Direct artist/label clearance

ALLEGRO artists should sign a radio/webcasting clearance covering at least:
- ownership/control of the master recording
- authority to grant the licence
- territory
- internet radio/webcasting right
- simulcast/archiving/podcast rights if required
- promotional clips
- term
- royalty treatment
- metadata and reporting
- explicit-content classification
- warranties and indemnities
- takedown/dispute procedure

Direct artist permission does **not automatically replace** SAMRO/SAMPRA licences where those collective rights remain administered by them. Confirm repertoire and mandates before assuming a direct licence is sufficient.

## 5. Outside music

Do not ingest tracks from Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, consumer downloads or another consumer service.

For outside artists/labels, obtain:
- direct master licence or label/aggregator agreement
- composition/performing-right coverage
- territory rights
- webcasting right
- artwork/name/likeness permission where used
- metadata/ISRC and ownership information

Mark a track `rights_status=verified` and `radio_clearance=cleared` only after documentary evidence exists.

## 6. Advertising and sponsorship

Ad sales agreements should state:
- advertiser legal entity and contact details
- campaign dates
- creative supplied/approved
- rate and VAT treatment
- number/type of spots
- make-good rules
- proof-of-play reporting
- cancellation rules
- prohibited/regulated categories
- advertiser warranties that claims are lawful and substantiated
- ALLEGRO right to reject/suspend creative

Advertising should comply with the Advertising Regulatory Board Code of Advertising Practice and any sector-specific law (for example financial services, medicines, gambling, alcohol, tobacco/nicotine, competitions or political advertising).

Clearly distinguish paid advertising/sponsorship from editorial/programme content.

## 7. Privacy and listener data

Comply with POPIA for listener accounts, analytics, advertiser leads, competitions, newsletters and direct marketing.

Minimum controls:
- privacy notice
- purpose limitation and minimal collection
- processor/operator contracts where applicable
- secure retention/deletion policy
- access/correction/objection workflow
- breach-response process
- opt-out mechanism for electronic marketing
- proof of consent where consent is required

Do not sell listener personal information to advertisers.

## 8. Terms and moderation

Publish:
- listener terms
- privacy notice
- cookie/analytics notice if applicable
- creator/upload terms
- advertiser terms
- competition rules where applicable
- complaints/takedown contact
- content standards and explicit-content policy

## 9. Corporate separation

ALLEGRO-VIBEZ should have its own:
- contracting entity
- bank/payment records
- advertiser invoices
- rights licences
- creator contracts
- data records
- tax treatment
- liabilities and insurance

Shared IZAKHONO infrastructure remains a service provider to ALLEGRO rather than commingling records with other group entities.

## 10. Launch gate

Do not represent ALLEGRO Radio as live until:
- [ ] ICASA internet-only position confirmed for the current architecture
- [ ] SAMRO licence/coverage confirmed
- [ ] SAMPRA licence/coverage confirmed
- [ ] first 24+ hours of music is rights-cleared
- [ ] station-ident fallback audio exists
- [ ] stream endpoint has passed a continuous 24-hour test
- [ ] live presenter ingest authentication is tested
- [ ] advertiser contract and creative approval workflow are ready
- [ ] playlist/proof-of-play logs are retained
- [ ] POPIA privacy/marketing controls are live
- [ ] legal entity, invoicing and VAT treatment are confirmed
