# ALLEGRO RADIO — ARTIST / MASTER WEBCAST CLEARANCE HEADS OF TERMS

Use this as the commercial instruction sheet for counsel to convert into the final artist/label agreement.

## Parties
- ALLEGRO-VIBEZ operating entity
- Artist / label / master owner

## Required grant
The rights holder grants ALLEGRO a non-exclusive right, for the agreed territory and term, to:
- transmit the cleared master recording through ALLEGRO's internet radio/webcasting service
- include the master in scheduled and automated programming
- identify the artist, track, album/release and contributors
- use supplied artwork and approved publicity material to promote the station and track
- create short promotional excerpts where agreed
- log and report plays to rights organisations and rights holders

## Rights-holder promises
The rights holder confirms:
- it owns or controls the rights being granted
- contributor/performer consents required from it have been obtained
- metadata supplied is accurate
- no conflicting exclusive grant prevents ALLEGRO's use
- it will notify ALLEGRO promptly of a rights dispute

## Fields ALLEGRO must capture
- contract/clearance reference
- artist legal name
- stage name
- label/master owner
- track title
- ISRC
- composers/authors/publishers
- release date
- explicit-content flag
- territories
- start/end date
- permitted uses
- royalty/payment terms
- takedown contact

## Collective-rights reservation
The agreement must state that ALLEGRO remains responsible for any licences/royalties payable to SAMRO, SAMPRA or other collecting societies to the extent those rights are not directly granted or cannot lawfully be waived by this agreement.

## Takedown
ALLEGRO may immediately suspend a track on credible notice of infringement, rights conflict, court order, regulator direction or breach of platform rules while the dispute is reviewed.
