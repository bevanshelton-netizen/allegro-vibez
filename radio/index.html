THE STATION

One radio station.
One signed roster.

ALLEGRO VIBEZ Radio exists to give ALLEGRO-signed artists a dedicated broadcast platform. The music policy is intentionally exclusive: no open submissions, outside catalogue or third-party tracks enter the rotation. Every track must be linked to an active ALLEGRO artist, approved for broadcast and cleared for the intended use.

● PRE-LAUNCH · STREAM NOT CONNECTED
ALLEGRO VIBEZ Radio

The station player will activate when the continuous broadcast stream and approved roster playlist are connected. We will not simulate a live broadcast or play unapproved demo material.

Broadcast eligibility: active signed ALLEGRO artist + approved release/track + confirmed master and broadcast permission + working audio asset. Artist profile status alone is not sufficient.
