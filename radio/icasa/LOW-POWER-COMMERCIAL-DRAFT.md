## Nature of service

ALLEGRO Radio is a commercial music, entertainment, culture and creator-development service targeting a broad contemporary audience. The service will carry a predominantly music-led schedule supported by presenter links, creator interviews, lifestyle information, sport/culture features, community-interest information and controlled commercial messaging.

## Programming

ALLEGRO operates 24 hours per day using a hybrid live/autopilot model.

Core shows:

- The Love Lounge

## South African creative-economy contribution

ALLEGRO will:
- create structured exposure for rights-cleared South African artists;
- provide a submission/review pathway for emerging creators;
- preserve ownership and contributor metadata;
- use split/clearance checks through ARTIST PROTECT;
- provide proof-of-play records;
- develop presenting, production and radio-business skills through ALLEGRO Radio Academy;
- make entry-level advertising inventory accessible to SMEs.

## Advertising approach

Commercial inventory is limited by format clocks to protect listener experience. Campaigns require approval before playout. Proof-of-play is recorded for reporting and billing.

## Music rights

No track is eligible for automated playout unless its rights status and radio clearance are approved with a clearance reference. The applicant will maintain appropriate performing-right, needletime/neighbouring-right and other licences required for the music actually broadcast.
