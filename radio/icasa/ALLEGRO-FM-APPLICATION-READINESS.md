# ALLEGRO RADIO — ICASA FM APPLICATION READINESS PACK

Prepared: 8 September 2026

## Applicant concept

**Proposed applicant:** Izakhono Africa (Pty) Ltd or a dedicated compliant broadcasting subsidiary, subject to final legal/entity review.

**Proposed service:** ALLEGRO Radio — commercial, music/culture/creator-development sound broadcasting service.

**Primary commercial model:** advertising, sponsorship, branded content subject to broadcasting rules, artist/creator commercial services and digital extensions.

**Current service:** online radio under development. An online stream does not itself create an FM spectrum right.

## Recommended licensing path

### Path A — Low-Power Commercial Sound Broadcasting

This is the immediately actionable class-licence route to investigate for a geographically limited commercial FM service.

ICASA describes low-power commercial sound broadcasting as a class broadcasting licence category. The current 2026/27 initial administrative fee is R8,473.

Before filing, obtain/confirm:
- exact intended coverage site and use case;
- eligibility under the Low-Power Sound Broadcasting framework;
- available/assignable frequency;
- transmitter/site coordinates;
- antenna height and pattern;
- ERP/transmitter power;
- interference analysis;
- equipment type approval;
- property/site permission;
- company and ownership documents.

### Path B — Individual Commercial FM

A conventional individual commercial sound broadcasting licence is not an always-open application. ICASA issues an **Invitation to Apply (ITA)** and applications are made in response to that ITA.

Therefore ALLEGRO should maintain a completed ITA-ready commercial broadcasting dossier and respond when ICASA opens suitable commercial FM spectrum.

### B. Service proposition
- station name: ALLEGRO Radio

- target audience
- geographic market
- languages
- differentiation from existing services
- public-interest contribution
- local music/creator development

### C. Programming
Attach:
- 24/7 programme schedule
- show descriptions
- format clocks
- music policy
- local/SA/African music strategy
- emerging artist policy
- children's/sensitive-hours policy where relevant
- advertising load policy

### D. Rights/compliance
- SAMRO performing-right licensing plan

### F. Financial

- staffing
- music-rights/licensing
- marketing

Prepare evidence on:

- youth/emerging creator opportunities

## Draft motivation

ALLEGRO Radio seeks to establish a commercially sustainable South African sound broadcasting service that combines compelling entertainment with measurable creator development. Its programming strategy gives meaningful exposure to rights-cleared South African and African music, creates structured pathways for emerging artists, supports advertiser access for SMEs, and uses transparent rights and proof-of-play systems.

The station's operating design combines professional live presentation with resilient automated playout, rights-controlled catalogue selection, commercial-load limits, authenticated live contribution and auditable proof of transmission.

ALLEGRO intends to compete on listener value and creative-economy development rather than simply duplicate an existing music format.

## Frequency request language

Do **not** invent an FM frequency in the application.

Use:
"ALLEGRO requests consideration for a suitable FM frequency within the frequencies lawfully made available by the Authority for the applicable licensing process and proposed coverage area, subject to coordination and technical feasibility."

## Current regulatory constraint

The latest ICASA material reviewed on 8 September 2026 shows that individual commercial sound broadcasting applications are ITA-driven. No assumption is made here that a suitable individual commercial FM ITA is currently open.

The application pack is therefore maintained in two forms:
1. low-power commercial readiness; and
2. ITA-ready individual commercial FM dossier.

## Filing gate

Do not submit until:
- correct applicant entity chosen;
- current ICASA form/process confirmed;
- coverage area chosen;
- RF engineer has completed technical annexure;
- board resolution signed;
- current fee confirmed and paid;
- all required hard/electronic copies prepared;
- all declarations signed.
