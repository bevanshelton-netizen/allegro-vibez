# ALLEGRO PUBLIC / LOW-COST MUSIC POLICY

ALLEGRO may use a recording as low-cost launch catalogue only when the **recording itself** and the underlying work are cleared for the actual planned use.

## Automatically acceptable licence classes, subject to evidence

- CC0 / explicit public-domain dedication;
- verified public-domain sound recording + verified public-domain composition in the relevant territories;
- CC BY where commercial use and sharing/public performance are covered and attribution is fulfilled;
- CC BY-SA where commercial use is permitted and ALLEGRO can comply with the applicable attribution/share-alike obligations for adaptations;
- a paid licence that expressly covers the intended online/international radio use.

## Not acceptable for commercial ALLEGRO radio without separate permission

- CC BY-NC / any NonCommercial licence;
- personal-use-only / creator-use-only licences;
- generic "royalty free" wording without the actual licence;
- a public-domain composition paired with a copyrighted modern recording;
- music copied from YouTube, Spotify, TikTok, SoundCloud or another streaming service merely because it is publicly accessible;
- licences that cover online videos/podcasts but exclude online radio.

## Launch source strategy

1. **CC0/public-domain recordings with independent evidence** — first choice.
2. **Direct artist licences through ALLEGRO** — preferred as creator onboarding grows.
3. **CC BY / CC BY-SA recordings** — use with an attribution ledger and visible/on-air credits where appropriate.
4. **Low-cost catalogue licences** — buy only when the grant expressly covers ALLEGRO's actual broadcast scope.
5. **Mainstream repertoire** — only after the required collecting-society/label/master licensing is active.

## Source notes

Musopen states that it provides recordings believed to be public domain, but its terms and FAQ also warn users to independently verify copyright status; therefore Musopen is a discovery source, not automatic clearance.

FreePD-origin CC0 material may be useful where the specific recording's CC0/public-domain evidence is captured and retained.

Jamendo's 2026 licensing terms distinguish online/project licences from radio rights. National radio and international radio require broader licence levels; standard online licences are not enough.

## Evidence required in ALLEGRO

Every externally sourced launch track must record:
- track and artist;
- source URL;
- sound-recording licence;
- composition status/licence;
- territory;
- commercial-use permission;
- radio/broadcast permission;
- attribution text if required;
- evidence capture date;
- immutable licence/evidence reference.

"Public on the internet" is never a rights status.
