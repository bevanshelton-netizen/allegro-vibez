# ALLEGRO RADIO ACADEMY — QCTO / MICT SETA ACCREDITATION PLAN

## Target registered qualification

**SAQA ID:** 122622  
**Title:** Higher Occupational Certificate: Radio and Multimedia Content Practitioner  
**NQF Level:** 5  
**Credits:** 145  
**Curriculum Code:** 352105-001-00-00  
**Quality Council / Accreditor:** QCTO  
**Assessment Quality Partner:** MICT SETA

## Structural decision

Do not attempt to market the existing eight ALLEGRO Radio Academy pathways as separate accredited qualifications.

Instead:

1. Use the registered **Higher Occupational Certificate: Radio and Multimedia Content Practitioner** as the accredited flagship programme.
2. Map ALLEGRO's existing theory, practical, live-station and technical content into the official curriculum.
3. Retain specialist ALLEGRO pathways (DJ/live, advertising, rights, station management, etc.) as:
   - enrichment modules,
   - optional internal specialisations,
   - short non-accredited programmes,
   - or future QCTO skills-programme applications where appropriate.
4. Use ALLEGRO Radio as the real workplace environment for the required work-experience component, under a formal workplace MoU.
5. Keep all public marketing clearly marked **accreditation pending** until a final QCTO accreditation letter is issued.

## Provider structure

Recommended clean model:

**Accredited Skills Development Provider**
- Edu-Build Institute / another dedicated training legal entity, if current legal/provider status supports the application.

**Workplace partner**
- ALLEGRO-VIBEZ Radio.

This preserves the group's separate-entity rule:
- provider contracts and learner records sit with the accredited SDP;
- ALLEGRO supplies workplace exposure, studio practicals and work-experience evidence;
- an intercompany/workplace MoU defines responsibilities.

If ALLEGRO itself is chosen as the SDP, it will need to satisfy all QCTO provider requirements independently.

## QCTO accreditation evidence pack

Prepare:

1. CIPC registration / proof of establishment.
2. Current Tax Compliance Status.
3. Facilitator/assessor CVs, certified IDs and qualifications.
4. Evidence that facilitators/assessors hold comparable or higher qualifications required by the curriculum.
5. Financial statements if operating >1 year, or business plan if <1 year.
6. Valid OHS audit report.
7. Lease or ownership proof for the training premises.
8. Signed workplace MoU covering all official work-experience modules.
9. Programme Implementation Plan.
10. Learning Material Matrix.
11. Knowledge-component equipment/inventory list.
12. Practical-component equipment/inventory list.
13. Fully aligned learner guides, facilitator guides and assessment materials.
14. Training organogram.
15. Quality Management System and implementation controls.
16. Learner Management Information System.
17. Relevant governance, assessment, moderation, RPL, appeals, complaints, POPIA, OHS and certification policies.
18. Site-readiness evidence for studio, computers, internet, microphones, mixers, DAW, streaming and production equipment.

## Assessment structure

ALLEGRO's internal theory tests and practical rubrics are useful formative/summative evidence, but they do not replace the official external assessment.

Learners must complete:
- official knowledge modules,
- official practical modules,
- required workplace modules,
- statements of results,
- workplace record / portfolio of evidence,
- and the External Integrated Summative Assessment (EISA) under the MICT SETA AQP framework.

## Site model

QCTO accreditation is site-specific. Even for virtual delivery, the provider must have a physical South African address.

If later training is delivered from multiple campuses/sites, each site may require its own accreditation/site approval route.

## Immediate work programme

### Phase A — Alignment
- obtain the official curriculum document for 352105-001-00-00;
- build a line-by-line matrix;
- restructure current Academy modules to the official module codes;
- create missing learning material;
- preserve ALLEGRO specialisations as enrichment.

### Phase B — Provider readiness
- choose the legal SDP entity;
- lock the training site;
- appoint qualified facilitators/assessors;
- complete OHS, QMS, LMIS and policy pack;
- execute the ALLEGRO workplace MoU.

### Phase C — Application
- submit the QCTO online SDP accreditation application;
- upload compliance documents when invited;
- complete desktop evaluation;
- prepare Programme Implementation Plan and Learning Material Matrix;
- undergo QCTO programme-delivery readiness/site visit.

### Phase D — Launch after approval
- activate accredited enrolment;
- register learners correctly;
- deliver knowledge + practical + workplace components;
- prepare learners for MICT SETA/QCTO external assessment;
- retain all assessment and workplace evidence.

## Core principle

**ALLEGRO Radio Academy should become a real accredited work-integrated radio school, not merely a short-course portal.**

The live radio station is a strategic advantage because the official qualification requires practical skills and workplace evidence in radio and multimedia production.
