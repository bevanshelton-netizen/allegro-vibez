# ALLEGRO RADIO ACADEMY

## Purpose

ALLEGRO Radio Academy is the talent-development arm of the station.

It trains the people who will operate, present, sell, produce, engineer and govern the network. The model deliberately connects **training -> supervised practical -> live station opportunity -> portfolio -> employment/freelance opportunity**.

## Training pathways

1. Radio Presenting & On-Air Performance
2. Radio Production & Programming
3. Internet Radio Technical Operations
4. Radio Advertising & Sponsorship Sales
5. Music Rights, Compliance & Logging
6. Interviewing, Newsroom & Storytelling
7. DJ, Live Sessions & Artist Showcases
8. Station Management & Autopilot Operations

## Delivery model

- self-paced lessons
- tutor-led masterclasses
- studio simulations
- supervised ALLEGRO Radio practical shifts
- live-show observation
- advertising sales simulations
- technical incident drills
- rights/compliance logging
- portfolio evidence

## Talent pipeline

Top learners can move into:

- trainee presenter slots
- weekend/overnight shows
- producer assistant roles
- ad-sales internships
- technical operations shifts
- regional/country programme teams
- KORA / ALLEGRO media opportunities

Completion must never automatically guarantee employment.

## Certification boundary

Until a formal accreditation or recognised partnership is secured, learners receive an **ALLEGRO Radio Academy Certificate of Completion / Competence**, not a SAQA/QCTO-accredited qualification.

If accreditation is pursued later, the current curriculum can be mapped to the relevant South African occupational or skills-programme standards before marketing it as accredited.

## Commercial model

The academy can support:

- free introductory training to grow the talent pool
- paid advanced programmes
- sponsored youth cohorts
- advertiser-funded bursaries
- corporate/media training
- campus partnerships
- practical placement programmes
- presenter/DJ masterclasses

Never advertise a programme as accredited, registered or nationally recognised unless documentary proof exists.
