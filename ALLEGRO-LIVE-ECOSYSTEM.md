# ALLEGRO LIVE — Live Entertainment Ecosystem

## Purpose

ALLEGRO LIVE is the live-entertainment layer of ALLEGRO-VIBEZ. It extends the creator business from recorded music into performances, events, experiences, audience commerce and event-derived media.

The source material supplied for this direction is treated as industry inspiration only. ALLEGRO uses the useful business categories and workflow concepts, but does not reproduce third-party branding, named-company positioning, proprietary copy or artwork.

## Commercial architecture

ALLEGRO LIVE is designed around multiple value layers rather than ticket sales alone:

1. Ticketing and access
2. VIP and hospitality
3. Sponsorship and brand partnerships
4. Merchandise
5. Venue and ancillary services
6. Streaming and digital extensions
7. Event content and media licensing
8. Fan memberships and communities
9. Artist booking and management services
10. Data and audience intelligence

## Core workflow

`CONCEPT → TALENT → VENUE → PRODUCTION → TICKETING → AUDIENCE → EVENT → CONTENT / COMMERCE`

Each stage should eventually have explicit owners, statuses, permissions and financial/rights records.

## ALLEGRO product relationship

`DISCOVERY → ARTIST OS → CREATOR STUDIO → RELEASES → RIGHTS → WALLET → ALLEGRO LIVE`

ALLEGRO LIVE must not become a separate disconnected event website. It is another commercial surface of the creator operating system.

An artist should be able to move from a release to a live opportunity; a promoter should be able to discover suitable artists; an audience member should be able to discover an event; and approved event activity should feed legitimate commerce, content and rights records back into the platform.

## Integrity gates

ALLEGRO must never fabricate:
- ticket sales
- attendance
- event revenue
- sponsorship commitments
- artist fees
- royalties
- event status
- venue availability

Commercial values become factual only when supported by the relevant transaction, contract, operational or settlement record.

## Infrastructure principle

ALLEGRO LIVE follows the same IZAKHONO-owned-first infrastructure directive as the rest of ALLEGRO-VIBEZ. External ticketing, payment, streaming or event-service providers may be adapters where necessary, but the platform's canonical creator, event, rights and commercial records remain under the IZAKHONO architecture.
