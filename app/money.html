TRANSPARENCY PRINCIPLE
Every deduction must have a contractual basis. International clients pay through the ALLEGRO-managed booking workflow. Cleared funds are reconciled, authorised deductions are recorded, artist settlement is calculated, and the resulting payment is reflected in the Artist Wallet and statement. Pass-through travel and per-diem amounts remain separately identifiable. ALLEGRO does not treat artist funds as discretionary revenue, and no earnings, balances or payment events are fabricated.
