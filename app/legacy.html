RIGHTS-SAFE OPERATING RULE

ALLEGRO-VIBEZ does not automatically acquire masters, publishing or catalogue ownership. Promotional use, catalogue preparation and commercial opportunities remain distinct from rights administration. Any licensing, management, ownership or monetisation agreement must be explicit and verifiable.
