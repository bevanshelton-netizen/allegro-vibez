RIGHTS-SAFE RULE

ALLEGRO-VIBEZ does not automatically acquire masters, publishing or catalogue ownership. Catalogue preparation and promotion are separate from rights administration. Commercial use proceeds only after explicit, verifiable rights and agreements. Pipeline states are operational records, not proof of payment, ownership, licensing clearance or regulatory approval.
