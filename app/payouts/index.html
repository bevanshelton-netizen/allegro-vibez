International bookings

For ALLEGRO-managed international work, the promoter/client pays through the designated ALLEGRO booking workflow. Funds are verified and reconciled before settlement. Artists should not be asked to bypass the agreed management and payment process.

Financial integrity

ALLEGRO records and controls the commercial workflow but does not invent balances or payment events. Actual movement of money must occur through an authorised payment or banking provider. Contractual deductions must be identifiable, and artist funds must not be treated as discretionary ALLEGRO revenue.
