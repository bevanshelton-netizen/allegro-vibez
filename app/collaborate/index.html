GLOBAL WORK

Cross borders. Stay protected.

International opportunities generated through ALLEGRO are routed through ALLEGRO MANAGEMENT for commercial negotiation, contracts, travel coordination and settlement.

ALLEGRO Collaborate creates the connection. ALLEGRO Management takes qualifying international work through the professional booking process. Commercial terms are agreed privately and recorded in the appropriate deal documentation.
