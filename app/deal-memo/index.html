Rules of engagement

A Deal Memo does not replace the final booking agreement. No performance should be treated as contractually executed merely because an opportunity or application was accepted. International opportunities originating through ALLEGRO remain subject to ALLEGRO Management. Jurisdiction-specific legal review should be obtained where required.
