THE MONEY ENGINE

We make money when the artist makes money.

Artist Wallet — gross income, external costs, platform/service share, approved expenses and artist net are separated and visible.
Artist ownership — ALLEGRO-VIBEZ does not automatically take ownership of recordings, compositions, brands or other intellectual property. Ownership is governed by explicit written agreements.
Statements — every monetised activity should produce a clear transaction record and statement as the financial infrastructure is integrated.
Third-party money movement — regulated payment, custody, escrow and payout functions remain with appropriately licensed partners until verified integrations are connected.
